Health

Food

Who

When possible, with other people. Otherwise, let your mind wander, and use it as a moment to slow down.

What

Satiety
  • Fiber
  • Protein
  • Fat
Health
  • Whole grains
  • Unsaturated fat
  • Vegetables (and micronutrients)

Avoid greasy foods.

Where

Eat in third spaces without distractions.

When

Eat if and when you are hungry. Don’t eat because it is time to eat, although you will find that if you are used to eating at consistent times, you will naturally feel hungry then.

Why

Eat because you enjoy the flavor of the food, and because you feel good by eating the food. Don’t eat out of boredom or emotionally.

How

Eat until you are no longer hungry. Focus on the physical sensation of not wanting to eat more, not on the desire to taste the flavor again. Put down your phone, your book, your work. Get rid of distractions. Eat slowly, and intentionally. Most importantly, be aware that you can always save the food, or come back to get more. You don’t need to eat every calorie, since you pay for the experience of the food, not for the food itself.
